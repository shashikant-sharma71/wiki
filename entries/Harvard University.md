Harvard University, founded in 1636 in Cambridge, Massachusetts, is the oldest and one of the most prestigious universities in the United States and the world. As a member of the Ivy League, it is renowned for academic excellence, groundbreaking research, and a vast network of influential alumni including Barack Obama, Mark Zuckerberg, and Bill Gates. Harvard offers top-ranked programs in law, business, medicine, computer science, and more through its 13 schools. With an acceptance rate of around 3–4%, admission is highly competitive, but generous financial aid makes it accessible to students globally. Its historic campus, vibrant student life, and global partnerships make Harvard a leader in education, innovation, and leadership development.
